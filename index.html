About Atlas

Atlas Management Consulting is a California corporation founded to help practices — and businesses more broadly — improve productivity and efficiency without piling on complexity. We believe most organizations already have what they need to succeed; the job is to remove the friction in the way.

Questions, answered

What exactly does Atlas do?

We help practices run better and collect more of what they've already earned — tightening scheduling, intake, and workflow, and strengthening billing and the revenue cycle so charges get captured and denials get fixed at the root. Practical, hands-on work alongside your team, not a binder of theory.

How does an engagement start?

With a free, no-obligation conversation about where things feel stretched. From there we scope a focused assessment, share what we find, and propose the highest-leverage fixes first — you decide how far to take it.

Do you only work with medical practices?

Healthcare is our deepest expertise, but the underlying work — removing friction, capturing revenue, building systems a team can sustain — applies to most small and mid-sized businesses.

How is Atlas Dictation different from cloud dictation tools?

It runs entirely on your own Mac. Your voice is transcribed on the device and never sent to a server, which sidesteps the privacy and PHI exposure that come with cloud dictation. It's a one-time download — no account, no subscription.

Where are you based?

San Jose, California. We work with practices across California.
