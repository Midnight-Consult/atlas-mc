Great care shouldn’t be held back by messy operations

Most practices don’t lose time and money to one big problem — they lose it to a hundred small ones: a clunky schedule, a charge that never got captured, a claim denied for a fixable reason, a workflow everyone tolerates but no one owns. Atlas finds those leaks and closes them, so your team can focus on patients instead of paperwork.

Stop leaving money on the table

In healthcare, the difference between a healthy practice and a struggling one is often invisible: charges that never got billed, and claims denied for reasons that were entirely preventable. Atlas treats billing as a discipline, not an afterthought.

  • Capture every billable charge — nothing essential missed
  • Submit clean claims the first time
  • Reduce denials significantly at the root cause
  • Work denials and rejections back to full payment
  • Tighten coding, documentation, and eligibility checks
  • Shorten the time from visit to reimbursement

About Atlas

Atlas Management Consulting is a California corporation founded to help practices — and businesses more broadly — improve productivity and efficiency without piling on complexity. We believe most organizations already have what they need to succeed; the job is to remove the friction in the way.

The practice is led by Monika Tanwar, who brings an MBA in business management and hands-on experience running the operational and financial side of a practice. Our approach is grounded and practical: understand how the work truly flows, then make it flow better — measurably.
